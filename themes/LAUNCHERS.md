# Clean & Open-Source Android Launchers

Default OEM launchers (ColorOS Launcher, vivo Launcher) often come bundled with global search ads,
sponsored recommendation feeds, and non-removable discovery pages.

---

## 1. Liquid Glass Home (Built-in CleanYourPhone Launcher)
- **Best for:** Apple-inspired minimalist aesthetic with liquid frosted glass dock and zero telemetry.
- **Features:** Floating frosted bottom dock, minimalist thin typography, real-time offline search, fluid scale-spring touch physics, <15MB RAM usage.
- **Location:** In this repository under [`launcher/`](../launcher/README.md).
- **Install:** Run `./launcher/build-and-install.sh` or `.\launcher\build-and-install.ps1`.

---

## 2. Lawnchair 14
- **Best for:** Stock Pixel / Clean Android experience.
- **Features:** Material You dynamic theming, fluid gestures, custom icon packs, no telemetry.
- **Link:** [lawnchair.app](https://lawnchair.app) or [GitHub](https://github.com/LawnchairLauncher/lawnchair)

## 2. Niagara Launcher
- **Best for:** One-handed ergonomic flow and minimal distraction.
- **Features:** Vertical alphabet scroll list, integrated notification previews, lightning fast.
- **Link:** [niagaralauncher.app](https://niagaralauncher.app)

## 3. Neo Launcher
- **Best for:** Maximum privacy and open-source customization.
- **Features:** Built on modern AOSP Launcher3, native F-Droid support, zero proprietary trackers.
- **Link:** [GitHub](https://github.com/NeoApplications/Neo-Launcher)

## 4. Olauncher
- **Best for:** Extreme digital detox & text-based minimalism.
- **Features:** Clean plain-text list of apps, completely free and open-source.
- **Link:** [GitHub](https://github.com/tanujnotes/Olauncher)

---

## Setting a Default Launcher
1. Install your preferred launcher from F-Droid, GitHub, or Play Store.
2. Go to **Settings > Apps > Default Apps > Home app**.
3. Select your new launcher.
