# CleanYourPhone Themes & Aesthetic Customization

In addition to stripping background bloatware and trackers, CleanYourPhone includes guides
and recommendations to transform your smartphone into a clean, minimalist, distraction-free device.

---

## What's Included

| Guide | Description |
|---|---|
| **[Open-Source Launchers](LAUNCHERS.md)** | Replace heavy OEM home screens with clean, privacy-respecting launchers like Lawnchair 14, Niagara, or Neo Launcher. |
| **[Private DNS Ad-Blocking](PRIVATE-DNS.md)** | Block 100% of in-app ads and tracker telemetry at the operating system level without installing any battery-draining VPN app. |
| **[The Fossify Suite](FOSSIFY-SUITE.md)** | Clean, lightweight open-source replacements for stock bloated Gallery, Dialer, Messages, and Calendar apps. |

---

## The Minimalist Philosophy

1. **No Ad Injections:** No lock screen feeds, no notification tray ads, no sponsored suggestions.
2. **Instant Responsiveness:** Zero animation stutter or background memory pressure.
3. **Longer Battery Life:** Deep Doze sleep cycles with reduced idle drain.
